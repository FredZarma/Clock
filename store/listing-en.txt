Product name: ZTime
Short description: Free desktop clock with a 7-segment LED display.
Description:
ZTime is a free digital clock that sits on the desktop like an icon. It shows the PC time on a 7-segment LED display, stays above other windows, and does not appear in the taskbar.

Drag the clock to move it. Double-click to open the calendar. Right-click for the menu: language, alarm, size, color, seconds, date, 24-hour time, opacity, and the name under the icon.

Languages: English, Chinese, Hindi, Spanish, French, Russian, Japanese, and German.
